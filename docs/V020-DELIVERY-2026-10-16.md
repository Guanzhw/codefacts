## Public native performance and acceptance

The single `v020-published-fb3cc546ff1f-final1` campaign compared the actual
public Windows binary with published v0.1.18 on four fixed source corpora.
It used three cold observations per arm, 20 warm observations per case and
the frozen paired edit/restore method. OS caches were not purged.

| Failed target | Published v0.1.18 | Public v0.2.0 | Observed change | Frozen limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Go cold process-wall median | 5,459.0683 ms | 4,932.4502 ms | 9.6467% faster | ratio <=0.90 |
| globset map P95 | 13.0009 ms | 14.3190 ms | 10.1385% slower | ratio <=1.10 |
| globset outline P95 | 8.0696 ms | 9.4910 ms | 17.6143% slower | ratio <=1.10 |
| globset context-search P95 | 8.8360 ms | 11.7570 ms | 33.0579% slower | ratio <=1.10 |

Go cold missed its 10% target by 19.27073 ms. The warm increases were
1.3181, 1.4214 and 2.9210 ms respectively. Fresh closed Go state changed
from **192,663,552 to 155,963,392 bytes**, **19.0488% smaller**. The other
53 gates passed: fresh disk, the other three cold cases, four peak-memory
cases, 36/39 warm cases, seven Go absolute-latency checks and both paired
refresh cases. Existing allocated database files do not automatically shrink
to the fresh-state size.

All 24 cold sessions, 1,560 warm samples, 120 edits and 120 restores completed.
All source hashes/mtime restores, nine driver pins and 80 public material
descriptors passed post-timing checks. The 39 matched contract/validated fact
cases and source evidence passed, with 22,400 hashes and 87,600 lines checked
without evidence errors. Twelve continuation chains traversed 112 pages.
Full normalized response hashes remain unequal in six root-dependent cursor
cases; validated facts and actual same-root continuation passed separately.

Independent decision review recomputed all 120 relevant warm samples and six
Go cold values. Context search has multiple elevated samples. The cause is
unverified; neither noise nor an isolated source-code effect is established.
Root's [published decision](specs/mcp-simplification-20261015/published-release-decision-20261016.md)

independent post-timing audit passed integrity/correctness: all 2,150 manifest

owned-process closure. Earlier campaigns and decisions remain unchanged.
