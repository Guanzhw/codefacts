quality, exact token, lower-bound, allocation and failure-cost value. The adapter
first verifies the original projection, masks the seven clocks only in its reused
legacy arithmetic channel, recomputes, and asserts exact equality of every
non-time value. Original clocks are preserved in timing.rows[].clocks:
modelTurnMs, dshDurationMs, slotWallMs, preparationAndSandboxMs,
sourcePreparationMs, indexDurationMs, gradeDurationMs. Null remains unknown;
zero remains an observed zero. The original input files are immutable.

The map assigns every288 planned cell exactly once. Historical Context144 uses
the ROOT-approved serial closure. Original SWE pilot46 uses its original v2
origin's exact serial IDs, and the remaining pilot cells use their original
v2 reservation/timing/result/drained-wave receipts. V1 historical wave assembly

The closed inventory supplies only exact path/hash references. Historical v2
sources retain the accepted v1 contract. The new rolling chain follows the
controller's CONTRACT.md and source: root-approved schema3 decision/amendment,
pinned freeze/order/selectedCells, implementation artifact array, capacity,
ledger/pilot review, exact initial boundary and quality-only origin, owner and
successful exit, finalPins, verify-end all-eight-campaign closure, selected

The assembler does not read referenced model/metadata/source snapshots; their
ROOT acceptance and the controller's original auditors establish those facts.
It validates snapshot digests and protectedSnapshot succession as receipt data.

Journal replay reconstructs the exact issued, closed and accepted sets. A new
admission can use a slot only after the previous child has closed AND its result
has been accepted. It checks max2, one active campaign, earliest frozen eligible
selection, exact acceptedClosed/activeReservations, protected snapshot succession,
owner/decision/ordinal bindings, result acceptance after actual close, capacity
C20/D36GiB, exact selected48 and all children closed. Actual start/exit intervals
must fit the owning controller. Omitted/duplicate cells, foreign owners,
out-of-order journals and failed/unclosed chains fail at their owning contract.
No new SWE infrastructure exception is inferred. Fresh confirmation only is
supported; a midpilot switch, partial inherited confirmation or resume fails.

Rolling's configured maxConcurrency2 is separate from observed overlap. Each
job's interval is actual child spawn startedAt through process close finishedAt;
result.completedAt can precede close and cannot free a slot. For each job, clip
all positively overlapping worker intervals to its own interval and sweep their
start/end events; process ends before starts at equal timestamps. The resulting
peak is actualOverlapConcurrency. Thus one long worker may overlap many
sequential peers with peak2; touching endpoints and a serial singleton tail
have no positive peer overlap. overlapPeerCellIds retain all positively
overlapping peers, and activeAtAdmission retains the independent issuance state.
The public map repeats actual process-capacity/campaign checks from its closed
interval projection so portable arithmetic reproduction uses the same rule.

Historical wave comparisons use [conditionId, actual admitted-wave size] as
before. Rolling comparisons use [conditionId, actual-owned-worker-overlap-peak,
actualOverlapConcurrency]. Comparisons require identical model/cohort/split/task,
condition, mode and observed concurrency. Cross-condition/mode/concurrency pairs
retain both original clocks and null ratios with cross-timing-condition. Within
one stratum, descriptive treatment/ordinary ratios use only matched tasks. The
fixed same-condition task set and taskIds remain visible; missing clocks or
signed model-before infrastructure withhold a full-stratum ratio. A zero
individual baseline keeps its ratio unknown; a complete aggregate needs a
positive baseline total. Unpaired arms remain visible without invented matched
denominators. No time macro pools conditions or models. Operational overlap
strata do not establish equal load, isolation, throttling or a causal speedup.

Each execution interval uses owner.at through controller-exit.at, with an exact
exit evidence hash. Its elapsed value is the controller makespan observation
through exit receipt creation and excludes the subsequent final lock release.
Worker/model/DSH/setup/index/grade/slot clocks may overlap; their sums represent
work totals and are never substituted for controller elapsed time. Overlapping
controller intervals are never summed. Historical elapsed without evidence
remains unknown. Failed preparation history already includes canonical wall;
canonical, archived and full failed costs remain separately labeled and are not
added again to model or slot wall. Original quality/tokens/failure accounting
and the sole historical Context infrastructure exception remain unchanged.
